🌿 THE PROBLEM

A System Under Stress:
The Hidden Crisis in Livestock Healthcare

India’s livestock sector faces a silent crisis driven by disease burden, chemical dependency, and limited access to timely care directly impacting farmer income, animal health, and food quality.

Massive Economic Losses

Livestock diseases lead to thousands of crores in annual losses due to reduced milk yield, fertility issues, and increased treatment costs.

Antibiotic Dependency

Overuse of antibiotics is creating resistance, making treatments less effective and raising serious concerns around food safety and human health.

Limited Veterinary Access

A large percentage of rural farmers lack timely access to veterinary services, delaying diagnosis and worsening animal conditions.

Short-Term Treatment Model

Current approaches focus on symptom-based treatment rather than prevention, leading to recurring health issues and long-term inefficiency.

The need of the hour is a shift from chemical dependency to herbal, preventive, and sustainable livestock healthcare.
